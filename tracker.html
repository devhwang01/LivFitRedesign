Tracking Benefits

LivFit is designed to make workout and nutrition logging feel more organized, approachable, and easier to manage in one place.

  • Supports daily workout logging
  • Organizes nutrition entries clearly
  • Keeps recent activity easy to review
  • Encourages consistency through structure

Additional Features

The tracking experience can also support more advanced features that build on the same structure and keep the LivFit experience connected.

  • Meal search integration
  • Workout streak indicators
  • Progress charts
  • Saved templates for meals and routines
