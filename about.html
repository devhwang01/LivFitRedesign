About LivFit

LivFit was created as a fitness tracking concept focused on reducing the friction users often experience when trying to manage workouts, meals, and progress across multiple platforms.

The redesign builds on that original concept by improving the structure of the website, expanding interactivity, and moving the project toward a more complete multi-page experience.

The goal is to create a cleaner and more professional experience that better reflects the idea behind the app while also showing stronger front-end and UX thinking.

Project Focus

A more polished and interactive website redesign that better supports the LivFit concept and improves usability, structure, and visual consistency.

Audience

Students, beginner fitness users, and portfolio viewers looking at the project as an example of front-end design and development work.

Design Direction

Clean structure, stronger branding, better hierarchy, and more connected interactions that make the experience feel more intentional and user-friendly.
